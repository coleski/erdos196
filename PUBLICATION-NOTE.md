# Publication record

The proof was published on 14 September 2026 UTC.

This was not the first publication of the result. Boon Suan Ho submitted
[*A 4AP-free permutation of the positive integers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12780)
to arXiv on 11 September 2026. Ho's paper gives the prior full solution and
has priority. The work in this repository was developed independently and
published later; it makes no priority or novelty claim.
